# One-minute stochastic trend scalper

Branch: experiment/uptrend-vwap-only. Main and research/stocks-in-play-multistrategy are untouched. The existing Uptrend filter comparison workflow now runs the scalper on this branch rather than the old five-minute uptrend comparison. Select this branch and supply comma-separated stock symbols. Module: python -m penny_lab.stochastic_scalper --symbols SYMBOL1,SYMBOL2 --allocation 300.

Source: IG, Four simple scalping trading strategies, stochastic-oscillator section: https://www.ig.com/en/trading-strategies/four-simple-scalping-trading-strategies-190131. IG describes buying a bullish %K/%D crossover in a rising trend and exiting above 80 or on a bearish crossover. This implementation adapts that family to one-minute listed USD stocks, adding a rising SMA20 trend filter, an oversold threshold of 20, a recent-low stop and five-minute holding limit; it is not an exact replication of IG's three-minute market example.

Rules: fast %K over 14 completed candles, %D the three-period mean of %K; previous K <= D and current K > D with previous/current K reaching <=20; current close above SMA20, which is above its value three candles earlier. Entries 09:45-15:30 Eastern after 23 contiguous session candles. Signal orders execute next minute open with modeled costs. Stop 0.1% below the last three candle lows. Exit on K >=80 or bearish K/D crossover at next open, on stop, after five minutes at completed candle close, or at exchange session close. No historical-high target or hold-to-target behavior is used by this scalper. All indicators reset by session and after flat-history gaps.

Shared USD 2,000 account, USD 1-35 raw entry eligibility, USD 250/300 allocation ceiling, USD 20 cost-inclusive planned position risk and 1% prior-minute volume cap. Costs use the inherited 50 bps spread/25 bps slippage assumptions and a doubled-cost rerun, zero per-order fees. Stop gaps can exceed planned risk. Each cost run has an independent account, not one account per ticker.

Data: explicitly requests Yahoo period=7d and interval=1m, checks listed USD equity metadata, exchange calendar, every expected regular-session minute, positive OHLCV, duplicate candles and corporate actions. Incomplete current sessions, missing/zero-volume sessions and action days are excluded and logged. No synthetic minute candles or five-minute substitution. This requests recent data, not the old full-October evaluation. Empty coverage produces no fabricated backtest. An unresolved gap while holding raises an error.

Artifacts: coverage.json, hashed raw histories, SUMMARY.md, and per-cost run.json/trades.csv/equity.csv/rejections.csv. Trade records include source signal/entry times, bounded holding time, exit reason, planned risk, gross/net P&L and cost reconciliation.

Validation: 11 targeted tests passed in a local isolated harness, including positive setup, causal indicators, stops, next-open fills, five-minute exit, session liquidation, gaps and shared cash. Full repository suite, hosted workflow and provider acquisition have not been executed by the assistant. The workflow compiles all modules and runs the full suite before requesting data.
