# Active policy: USD 1-35 and pre-pullback high

Research diagnostics v2 on main now uses range_runner. This is the user's permitted SAME-DAY alternative, not an overnight or three-day implementation. Positions close at target, stop, or session end. The old experiment branch and legacy entry points retain their earlier settings.

Discovery uses inclusive Yahoo price bounds 1 and 35. Historical entry eligibility uses the RAW next-bar opening price in that inclusive interval; modeled execution costs may put the actual simulated fill outside that raw range. Signals outside the same range are blocked. All portfolios remain uptrend-only, with independent USD 2,000 accounts, selected USD 250/300 allocation ceiling, USD 20 planned risk, and existing A/B/C filters.

Previous high is defined deterministically as the highest high among the cfg.lookback=12 completed five-minute bars BEFORE the one-bar pullback and confirmation. This is a local pre-pullback range high, not a retrospectively confirmed multi-day swing pivot. It is timestamped and frozen at the signal, and must remain above the raw next opening price. Stop stays at the original structural pullback stop. Reward/risk is derived, not forced to 2:1: expected net reward is shares*(modeled adverse exit at target - modeled entry) minus both fees, divided by planned loss including adverse stop execution and both fees. Require at least 1.0 net RR. Gapped entries or insufficient reward are rejected, never supplied an invented higher target.

The structural target reference is stored in engine.run internally when the signal is emitted; it uses no subsequent bars. Cost-stress replay preserves the policy in provenance, recalculates sizing/net RR at each entry, and can reject trades that previously passed. Each trade's execution audit records target, reference rule/time, target freeze time, expected net reward, planned net risk and expected net RR. Target-policy tables append to the GitHub summary. Full audit is in run.json and execution-audit.csv. Realized RR can differ; stops do not guarantee realized losses.

Existing five-minute retrospective data-quality biases, simulated costs, no quote-depth model, immediate cash reuse, no certified point-in-time universe, no OTC simulation and no independently untouched validation remain. Legacy fixed-RR mode is retained for compatibility when no entry_policy is supplied; its reward_risk config field is not the target rule in this active workflow. The existing legacy universe label listed_under_10 is also compatibility-only; entry_policy records the actual bounds. Tests are included; assistant has not verified hosted tests or live provider runs.
