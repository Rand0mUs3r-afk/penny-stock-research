# New-only strategies and mixtures

Run Strategy discovery lab on main. The acquisition step no longer runs the old uptrend or A/B/C portfolios. It reuses the coverage/downloader pipeline but intercepts the first engine.run call BEFORE any strategy simulation and captures configuration/provenance only. The source has already saved history, hashes, references, eligibility and coverage at that boundary. A dedicated exception unwinds the old runner; all patched functions/arguments are restored. No fake zero-trade equity report is generated.

The root snapshot.json is acquisition metadata, not performance. A_baseline/run.json is a legacy snapshot-loader compatibility descriptor containing config/provenance and artifact_type=acquisition_only_metadata, strategy_execution_performed=false; it has NO summary, trades, or ending equity. The historical module/file labels do not mean an uptrend strategy was executed. Acquisition refuses to overwrite an existing baseline result. Use a fresh output directory.

Seven independently funded cases now run: opening-range breakout, failed-breakdown recovery, compression continuation, opening-range pullback hybrid, morning-ORB/afternoon-compression mix, breakout_recovery_mix (ORB plus failed breakdown), and breakout_recovery_compression_mix (ORB plus recovery plus compression). The last two are unions of qualified signals competing in one shared-cash portfolio per case, not three isolated equity curves added together. No duplicate concurrent ticker positions; overlapping signals are resolved by the engine score/tie rule. No automatic winner selection. Normal and doubled spread/slippage yield fourteen portfolio scenarios.

Existing $1-$35 inclusive raw price eligibility, October 1-31 defaults (exclusive November 1), independent USD 2,000 accounts, USD 250/300 allocation ceiling, USD 20 planned risk, no minimum RR, historical overhead targets and same-day exits remain. No target is invented. New rules and target/stop definitions remain as documented in STRATEGY_LAB.md. Inherited full-session/reference eligibility can still exclude data; this patch does not remove those safeguards or promise more trades.

The Actions summary shows acquisition coverage only, then new-case comparisons. Full trade tables and per-case attribution remain in the artifact strategy-lab folders. No uptrend backtest precedes them. Research diagnostics v2 and the experiment branch remain separate. No OTC execution, true quote calibration, point-in-time universe, independent holdout proof or live orders. These are hypotheses, not guaranteed profitable setups. Unit tests are included; assistant has not verified hosted execution or network results.
