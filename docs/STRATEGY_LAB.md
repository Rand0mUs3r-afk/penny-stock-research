# Strategy discovery lab

New manual workflow on MAIN; existing Research diagnostics v2 stays unchanged. Select Strategy discovery lab on main, October 1–31 defaults, USD 1–35, chosen allocation and existing credentials. It first runs the existing source acquisition/coverage pipeline, then replays the identical hash-checked eligible snapshot for five cases and spread/slippage multiples 1 and 2. Each case has an independent configured account. No automatic optimization or winner selection.

Cases: opening_range_breakout (first close above 9:30–9:45 range, >=1.5x preceding-bar mean volume, before/equal 11:30); failed_breakdown_recovery (sweep below prior 12-bar support, close recovery, following confirming close); compression_continuation (rising 8-bar impulse, 4-bar range <=50% impulse range, >=1.5x pause volume breakout); opening_range_pullback (range break then defended level/pullback and recovery); time_separated_mix (ORB until 11:30, compression noon to 15:00). Candle timestamps denote ends; entry stays next-bar open. ORB variants consume at most one emitted signal per ticker/day; a signal rejected later by entry/risk checks does not reset that allowance. Other setups retain the engine's no-duplicate-position rule and can reenter. Thresholds are predeclared experimental hypotheses, not proven optimal settings or exact academic replications.

Targets: nearest overhead previous complete exchange-session high or intraday strict pivot high confirmed by two completed bars to its right, all known at signal. Previous-session levels are suppressed across missing/action sessions. The same historical target is frozen until entry/exit; if next opening gaps at/above it, reject explicitly. No minimum RR threshold; risk/cash/stop checks still apply. No invented target when price is above all available historical levels. Stops use breakout-candle/pullback/sweep/consolidation structure. Same-day session exit.

Implementation uses scoped engine.scan and engine.previous_high adapters, restored after each run. Distinct strategy names are registered in Config without changing legacy four-strategy defaults. Engine references are keyed by symbol/signal time. Lab data still inherits the source pipeline's full-session and historical-reference eligibility; low sample counts may persist and must be read with source diagnostics. OTC is not simulated. Current symbols are not a historical universe. Costs are hypothetical, not measured quotes.

Summary: final equity, net P&L, trades, win rate, expectancy, drawdown, holding-duration upper bound, sample flags, target-unavailable counts, biggest winner, worst day, mean wins/losses. Each case has run.json, full report, trades, equity, setup decisions, rejections, strategy ranking, execution-cost audit and per-day/per-symbol attribution. These new results appear under Strategy discovery lab, separate from the preceding uptrend data-acquisition report. Artifact retains source data and universe hashes.

October is development data already inspected; lab results are not untouched out-of-sample validation. No-trade cases are inconclusive, not winners. Less than thirty trades is an operational warning only. Independent later-date testing and execution/cost verification are still required. Workflow may need substantial runtime for large historical universes. No live orders, no guaranteed profit. Tests are included but assistant has not verified hosted tests or network results.
