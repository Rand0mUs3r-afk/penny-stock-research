# Active policy: USD 1-35, previous high, no minimum RR

Research diagnostics v2 on main uses range_runner. Workflow evaluation defaults are 2026-10-01 inclusive and 2026-11-01 exclusive, representing October 1 through October 31, 2026. Warmup is requested before evaluation; missing sessions are reported, not invented. These are workflow defaults; direct CLI calls can still specify dates and blank CLI dates retain the underlying runner's relative-date behavior.

Minimum RR gating is disabled with minimum_net_rr=null. A trade is not rejected because its expected net RR is below 1:1. RR and expected net reward after modeled spread/slippage/fees are still calculated and reported. This includes nonpositive expected net reward when costs consume the move; no hidden positive-RR gate is substituted. Historical target must still exceed RAW entry, and stop/price/sizing/cash/data/planned-risk checks remain. An explicitly supplied positive minimum RR remains supported for legacy/research policies.

The historical target remains the highest high of the preceding 12 completed five-minute bars before the pullback and confirmation. It is fixed at the signal and never moved using future candles. Discovery and raw-entry prices must be USD 1-35 inclusive. Uptrend entries only, existing A/B/C filters, USD 2,000 starting equity, USD 250/300 allocation ceilings, USD 20 planned-risk limit, and SAME-DAY exits remain unchanged. This is not three-day or overnight holding.

Snapshot replay inherits entry_policy from provenance, so no-RR-gate behavior persists under cost stress and chronological reruns. Full target reference and RR records are in run.json and execution-audit.csv; summary includes the target-policy table. Earlier one-to-one policy documentation is superseded by this version. Main's Research diagnostics v2 is the active workflow; old branches/legacy entry points are not migrated. Existing retrospective-selection, quote-execution, universe and OTC limitations remain. Tests are included, but hosted tests and provider runs have not been verified by the assistant.
