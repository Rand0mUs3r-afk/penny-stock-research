# One active contextual setup: impulse first pullback

This replaces ALL engulfing, three-candle, stair-step, indicator and mixture cases in Strategy discovery lab. One active setup, impulse_first_pullback, runs at normal and doubled spread/slippage: two portfolio scenarios, not a search for a winner among mixtures. Legacy registered strategies remain for other workflows only. active-setups.json records the selected case; its historical removed list is not exhaustive.

On completed 5-minute raw-price bars, require an impulse closing above the preceding 12-bar high. Its range must be at least 1.5 times the median range of those preceding bars, bullish body at least 65% of its range, and close within the upper 20% of its range. Then require the first 1-3 candle pullback. Its first close must be below the impulse close; all pullback candles must remain above both the broken high and the impulse range midpoint, and none may trade above the impulse high. The final bullish confirmation must also hold those support levels and CLOSE above the impulse high. Stop 0.1% below the lowest pullback/confirmation low. No signals after 11:30 Eastern; at least 15 session bars needed. No EMA/RSI/Bollinger or candle-name-only trigger. A later bar cannot repeat the same impulse once a confirmation has already crossed its high. Separate later impulses can qualify.

This is a new hypothesis, not demonstrated improvement. Fewer trades or no trades are possible. Parameters are fixed, not optimized to October. I have not inspected an executed backtest proving either poor or superior performance. Synthetic tests cover positive confirmation, weak impulse, deep pullback, late confirmation, repeated signal and old-setup exclusion. Full repository tests and hosted workflow/provider results have not been run or verified by the assistant.

Preserved: USD 1-35 raw-price eligibility, October 1-31 defaults (November 1 exclusive), USD 2,000 account, USD 250/300 allocation ceiling, USD 20 planned risk, no minimum RR, same-day exits, known overhead historical-high targets and rejection when no valid target exists. A nearer historical target can still constrain a trade; this patch does not change that requested policy. Dollar-liquidity eligibility and volume-based ranking remain. Normal/stressed cost models and all coverage/full-session/current-universe biases remain. October remains development data. Data acquisition remains metadata-only, no old strategy backtest and no live orders.
