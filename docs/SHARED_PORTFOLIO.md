# One shared USD 2,000 backtest account

Active on experiment/uptrend-vwap-only via Uptrend filter comparison. The workflow now calls penny_lab.shared_monthly, not the independent monthly_trend comparison matrix. Main and the research branch remain unchanged. The removed 45-day strategy is not selected.

Exactly one cash ledger starts at USD 2,000. Buy-and-hold and monthly trend own separately attributed lots inside that ledger, not separate accounts. Every ticker and strategy draws from the same available cash; sales return funds to it. Default allocation targets are 50% monthly trend and 50% buy-and-hold; trend_weight changes that split without adding capital. Both strategies together share the USD 250/300 per-ticker purchase-allocation ceiling. The ceiling is not granted separately to each strategy. Price appreciation may take an existing buy-and-hold holding above the ceiling; new purchases are blocked while there is no room.

At initial allocation, the combined eligible allocation across five assets is at most USD 1,500 with a USD 300 ceiling, including entry costs, leaving at least USD 500 cash. Inactive monthly-trend allocations stay in shared cash. Buy-and-hold buys its initial allocation once; monthly trend adjusts its lots on completed month-end signals and executes next session. Buy-and-hold appreciation can change the actual strategy weight afterward; initial/target weights are not a continuous forced rebalance of the buy-and-hold lots. Unlevered, fractional adjusted-price ETF research units and the existing assets/signals/cost model remain as documented in MONTHLY_TREND.md.

There is ONE equity curve and ONE final equity, not a sum of independently funded strategy returns. The workflow runs only the selected cost assumption, normal by default. The earlier/later diagnostic periods are slices of the same curve: the later period starts with the earlier period's ending equity and carries its positions; there is no USD 2,000 restart. Attribution reports strategy P&L contributions, which reconcile to the shared account's net P&L.

SUMMARY.md and the GitHub run summary include a closed-trade table with strategy, ticker, exact entry trading date, exact exit trading date, units, calendar holding days and net P&L. A separate ticker holding ledger includes every closed FIFO segment and every still-open lot, with entry/exit/as-of dates and exact calendar age. Daily input prices support exact trading dates, not exact intraday fill times. Partial sells consume oldest lots within the same strategy/ticker and preserve original entry dates. Open positions have no fabricated exit date; their age is measured at the last observed date. The full artifact summary is retained if the hosted summary exceeds its size allowance.

Artifacts: run.json, SUMMARY.md, trades.csv, ticker_holding_days.csv, open_positions.csv, fills.csv, equity.csv, strategy_attribution.csv, periods.csv, signals.csv, coverage.json and hashed daily histories. Final equity includes cost-adjusted open positions, not just cash. Source histories and account constraints remain explicit research assumptions, not live fills.

CLI: python -m penny_lab.shared_monthly --years 10 --allocation 300 --trend-weight 50 --cost-multiple 1. The original monthly_trend comparison remains available manually for reproducibility, but is not the active shared-account workflow. Nine targeted shared-ledger/FIFO/report tests were run locally on synthetic data. Full repository tests, hosted execution and provider downloads have not been run by the assistant.
