# Active lab setups REPLACED

The active Strategy discovery lab on main now runs exactly five cases: ema_trend, bollinger_rsi, double_bottom, trend_reversion_mix, and three_family_mix. ORB, opening-range pullback, failed-breakdown recovery, compression continuation, uptrend pullback and VWAP reclaim are NOT active in this workflow. Their source code remains for historical experiments and regression compatibility. This is a replacement, not another extension of the prior active set. active-setups.json records the actual cases.

EMA entry: EMA8 crosses EMA21 from below on a completed candle, close above rising EMA34 (three-bar slope), preceding-mean volume confirmation, stop below last five-bar low. At least 35 session bars. Bollinger/RSI entry: prior close below its 20-period mean minus two population standard deviations, current close re-enters its current lower band, trailing-simple RSI14 was <=35 and improves, EMA21 is approximately flat (absolute three-bar slope <=0.3%); stop below prior/current low. At least 24 bars. Double bottom: two strict lows confirmed by two right-hand bars, separated 3-12 bars, lows within 0.75%, then completed close above their intervening neckline on >=1.25x preceding volume; stop below lower bottom. No future candles confirm an entry retrospectively. RSI is a simple trailing-change implementation, not Wilder-smoothed RSI. EMAs seed from the session's first close.

The trend/reversion mix combines EMA and Bollinger/RSI signals in one shared account. Three-family mix adds double-bottom signals. The common engine resolves concurrent signals and prevents duplicate ticker positions. Normal and doubled spread/slippage produce ten independent portfolio scenarios. Existing per-case reports retain distinct strategy attribution. All new entries stop after 15:00 Eastern and exit same day. Targets remain nearest historical overhead previous-session/intraday confirmed highs. No invented target or minimum RR requirement.

Stocks $1-$35 inclusive, October 1-31 (exclusive November 1), USD 2,000 account per case, USD 250/300 allocation ceiling, USD 20 planned risk, provider coverage and sample safeguards remain.
